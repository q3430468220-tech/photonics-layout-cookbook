photonics-layout-cookbook · unit devices

Device library

28 unit devices in three libraries. 6 are simulated so far; the others are drawn and planned.

This browser draws no WebGL; the devices are drawn below, one card each.

Point at a level or a device. Scroll, and the levels come together into one chip.

The 6 simulated devices at their true sizes, built from their GDS files and process_layers.csv; the dim drawings along the edges are the 14 planned silicon devices. Heights are stretched.

A device counts as done when it has a builder that passes the design-rule check, simulation scripts that load its GDS together with the process file, figures drawn from the kept results, and a chapter that explains the method, the numbers and the limits. The roadmap lists what comes next.

Choose a material or a solver to find the devices that use it.
