answer: the heat of the neighbours lowers the fidelity of the DFT to 1 - F = 1.1e-03 and of the random unitaries to a median of 1.1e-03.  A linear calibration of the heated waveguides brings the median to 2.8e-03: worse, because it undoes the heat that both arms of a block share.  A fit with every warmed waveguide in the model brings the median back to 3.0e-07 (largest 9.0e-07); without the heat it is 3.3e-07.

  arm only, and so leaves the difference.  The fit puts every site into the model and moves every heater a little.
