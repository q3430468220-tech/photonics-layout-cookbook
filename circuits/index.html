    The 4 × 4 mesh is drawn from its layout, with its rows 2.5 times further apart than they are. The brightness of a waveguide is the optical power in it, from the model of the chapter with the heater powers that its step 2 found; a heater glows with its power. The real matrix multiplier is a sketch of its layout and leads to its chapter; the planned circuits are sketches too.

    Four directions

    Each circuit is made of devices that have chapters of their own, and is simulated once its devices are. The programmable mesh comes first: optical computing builds on it.

    Programmable photonics

    4 × 4 Clements mesh

    Six interferometers and sixteen heaters that set any 4 × 4 unitary matrix of light.

    • Directional coupler
    • Thermo-optic phase shifter

    Optical computing

    Real matrix multiplier

    Two meshes and a column of attenuators: any real 4 × 4 matrix, by its singular value decomposition.

    built from

    • Directional coupler
    • Thermo-optic phase shifter
    simulated

    WDM microring link
